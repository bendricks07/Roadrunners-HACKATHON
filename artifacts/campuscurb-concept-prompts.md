## Satellite streets

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished, highly realistic product UI concept for CampusCurb, a parking map app for Cal Poly San Luis Obispo and nearby neighborhoods. The desired design language is the restraint and legibility of Apple Maps: system sans-serif typography, fine visual hierarchy, generous spacing, neutral frosted surfaces, restrained vivid blue actions. This is a generated design concept, with illustrative geography and parking estimates, not navigation data or measured occupancy.
The map is the product. Show green, warm amber and coral-red highlights on short individual curb segments with rounded ends and small natural breaks at junctions; keep the roadway interiors readable. Lots have subtle translucent area fills and refined perimeter outlines; parking garages use distinct P/garage symbols. Restriction markers are neutral gray locks, a separate visual channel from chance colors. Never show vacancy counts, percentages, prices, invented ETAs, payment status, or claims of live availability. No persistent sidebar, tab bar, navigation header, dashboard, marketing text, oversized filters, fake device bezel, hands, decorative 3D device, watermark, Google logo or Google imagery branding. All UI copy must be crisp, spelled correctly and readable at phone scale.
Asset type: One full-bleed portrait mobile app screen, approximately 390:844 aspect ratio, high resolution. A single screen, not a collage or presentation board.
Scene: Beautiful sharp overhead aerial imagery inspired by the campus edge of Cal Poly in San Luis Obispo: realistic low-rise campus buildings and sports field toward the upper third, tree-lined residential blocks in the middle and lower two-thirds. Close enough to see individual street blocks and parking lot layouts; do not waste half the viewport on distant hillside. Slightly subdued photographic tones let overlays and labels remain vivid.
Composition: The map occupies the entire screen. Readable restrained white map labels with a subtle dark halo: "Cal Poly", "San Luis Obispo", "Kennedy Library", "Foothill Blvd", "California Blvd", "Grand Ave", "Slack St", "Loomis St", "Fredericks St". Avoid crowded labels. Many short neighborhood curb segments have green/amber/red highlights, following both road edges, rather than long straight artificial grid lines. Two or three campus parking areas are tinted faint green, amber or muted coral; discreet parking and garage icons sit within them.
Controls: Two floating circular white glass buttons for Layers and location on the lower right with large touch areas. A compact white glass legend just above the bottom search field on the left, exact copy: "Demo parking estimates" and colored-dot labels "Good", "Fair", "Low". Beneath it, small legible "Check signs and permits". At the bottom, one large white glass rounded search field, 56-60 CSS px tall, with magnifying glass and exact placeholder "Where are you headed?". Leave safe area spacing so map information and footer do not overlap the search field. No selected-place sheet on this home screen. At the very bottom a discreet readable footer "CampusCurb · Design concept". Clean minimal mobile status bar at top.
Prioritize inviting map detail, confident typography, realistic curving geometry and immediately legible highlighted streets.
```
